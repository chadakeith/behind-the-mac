Data breaches keep coming, and [the latest one revealed by Cybernews involves 16 billion passwords](<https://cybernews.com/security/billions-credentials-exposed-infostealers-data-leak/>). There’s no way to know for sure if your passwords are included, though it’s always worth checking [Have I Been Pwned](<https://haveibeenpwned.com/>) and paying attention to monitoring features in password managers. Some media reports claim that passwords from accounts at Apple, Facebook, and Google were leaked, but this is likely overblown. While some accounts at those companies were compromised by infostealers, there were no centralized breaches. Our advice remains the same: use a password manager to ensure that each of your accounts has a strong, unique password and turn on two-factor authentication whenever possible.
