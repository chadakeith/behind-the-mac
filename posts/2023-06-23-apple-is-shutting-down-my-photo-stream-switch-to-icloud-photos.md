Apple has quietly announced that it is [shutting down the My Photo Stream service](<https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210705>), the company’s first iCloud-based effort at helping users sync photos between devices. [My Photo Stream](<https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201317>) had numerous limitations but was free and didn’t count against iCloud storage quotas. If you’re still using My Photo Stream, note that photos will stop uploading as of June 26, and all My Photo Stream photos will be removed from iCloud on July 26; they’ll remain on your individual devices. Apple is encouraging all remaining My Photo Stream users to switch to [iCloud Photos](<https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204264>), which is more capable in every way but might require you to pay for additional [iCloud+ storage](<https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201238>) to hold your photo library. Those who don’t want their photos in the cloud might check out [Mylio Photos+](<https://mylio.com/>). Contact us if you need assistance migrating to iCloud Photos or ensuring all your various photos are backed up securely.
