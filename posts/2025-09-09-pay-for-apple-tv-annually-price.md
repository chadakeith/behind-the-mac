Apple has raised the monthly subscription fee for Apple TV+ to $12.99, marking the third price increase since the streaming service launched at $4.99 in 2019. While the monthly cost has increased, Apple kept the annual subscription priced at $99, making it a more economical choice for those who plan to maintain their subscription throughout the year. Apple doesn’t put the annual billing option front and center on the Apple TV+ website, as you can see below, but you can easily switch to it in **Settings > _Your Name_ > Subscriptions** on your iPhone or iPad or in **System Settings > _Your Name_ > Media & Purchases > Subscriptions > Manage** on a Mac.
