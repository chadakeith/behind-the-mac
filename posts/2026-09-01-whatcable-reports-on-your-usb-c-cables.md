We’ve all ended up with a collection of random USB-C cables that arrived with devices, were purchased while traveling, or were left by visitors. But you can’t look at a cable to determine its data speed, how much power it can carry, or whether it’s hampering display quality. You could buy a [hardware tester](<https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYJL5Z67>),[ but for most p](<https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYJL5Z67>)eople, the free [WhatCable](<https://www.whatcable.uk/>) [is all yo](<https://www.whatcable.uk/>)u need. Just launch it and plug in a cable to learn what the cable can do and whether it might be causing performance or charging problems. If you need even more information, the £9.99 [WhatCable Pro](<https://www.whatcable.uk/pro>) [lets you name](<https://www.whatcable.uk/pro>) individual cables, troubleshoot flaky cables, and see what each port, cable, dock, adapter, and device supports and what was actually negotiated.
