Behind the Mac

Personal Mac notes from Chad Keith. Not a consulting pitch.

I'm Chad. I've spent most of my career in the weeds with Macs - home setups, small offices, and fleets that need to stay boring and reliable. This site is my personal voice on that stuff.

You'll find short posts here: how I set things up, what broke, what I'd do again, and the odd tip that saves someone a Friday night. No product brochure. No partner badges. Just notes from someone who lives in System Settings a little too often.

If you need the business side of Apple IT, that's Atlas - linked quietly below. This site stays personal.
