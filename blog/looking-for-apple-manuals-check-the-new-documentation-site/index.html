Looking for Apple Manuals? Check the New Documentation Site

Apple publishes a multitude of manuals and tons of technical documentation for its products on its support site, but until recently, it could be challenging to find some-thing specific because the search engine on Apple’s site is poor. For a better path into Apple’s online support materials, check out the company’s new Documentation site, which brings together manuals, specs, and some downloads for nearly all its prod-ucts. The operating system User Guides are particularly helpful, and they even pro-vide a Version pop-up menu that lets you make sure you’re getting information for the version you’re using.
